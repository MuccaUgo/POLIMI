High-frequency traps
Trap
Percentages need a valuation

A stake falling from 40% to 20% can be worth far more if the company grew from €10m to €60m. Never read dilution without the valuation behind it.

Trap
Post-money, not pre-money

An investor's share is the investment divided by the post-money valuation, which already includes the cash injected.

Trap
Primary ≠ secondary IPO

In a primary the cash goes to the company; in a secondary it goes to the selling shareholders.

Trap
SV = EV − debt + cash

Cash reduces net debt, so it raises shareholder value. Dropping the cash term is the usual slip.

Trap
Hostile refers to the board

A takeover is hostile when the target's board opposes it, whatever the price offered.

Trap
Unlimited ≠ unregulated

Unlimited companies lack limited liability but are exempted from filing accounts for public disclosure.

Trap
One-tier still supervises

A single board is not an absence of supervision: non-executives outnumber executives and some act as supervisors.

Trap
Stakeholder view adds, not replaces

The argument is that shareholder value alone is insufficient over the long term, not that it should be abandoned.

Trap
Deliberate ≠ intended

Deliberate strategy is only the part of the intended strategy actually carried out; the rest is unrealized. Realized strategy = deliberate + emergent.

Trap
Corporate picks, business plays

Which industries to be in is corporate strategy; how to compete inside one is business strategy. Both use the word competition.

Trap
Threats are never weaknesses

Strengths and weaknesses are internal by definition. A new entrant is a threat however badly it hurts.

Trap
Key success factor ≠ strength

What the industry demands is a key success factor; what the firm owns is a resource. External and internal analysis, not the same list.

Trap
Purpose is not a nicer mission

Mission says what the company does, vision what it wants to become, purpose why it exists at all.

Trap
Strategic needs all three

Long term effects, non-reversible effects and large resources. Non-reversible is the one people drop.

Trap
Tactics are not small strategy

They implement a strategy within resources it has already allotted. Strategy wins the war, tactics the single battle.

Trap
Strategy sits below politics

Politics derives the purpose, strategy works out how to reach it with the means available, tactics are the movements in battle.

Trap
Vision has two definitions here

General: the company's own future dream. Business-strategy process: industry foresight, how the business area will evolve — which competitors could share. Check which level the question is on.

Trap
External analysis is not about you

Its standpoint is the average generic player in the business area. Opportunities and threats are industry-wise, not firm-wise.

Trap
Realistic ≠ easy

The R in SMART asks for challenging but attainable. Too easy demotivates exactly as impossible does.

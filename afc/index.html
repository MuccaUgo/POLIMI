High-frequency traps
Trap
Accrual ≠ cash

Revenue belongs to the year the transaction takes effect; the cash inflow can fall in a different year.

Trap
FVTPL ≠ Available-for-sale

Both are measured at fair value, but the variation goes to the income statement for FVTPL and to the revaluation reserve for available-for-sale.

Today. Both are IAS 39 categories. Under IFRS 9 the second one is FVOCI, and its variation goes to other comprehensive income.

Trap
Impairment ≠ depreciation

Depreciation allocates cost across useful life; impairment captures a durable, unrecoverable loss of value.

Today. IAS 36 states it as carrying amount above recoverable amount, and the loss can be reversed later for assets other than goodwill.

Trap
Land is never depreciated

Every other item of PPE is, and depreciation continues even while an asset sits idle.

Trap
LIFO is not allowed

IFRS permits FIFO and weighted average only; LIFO logic is considered unrealistic.

Trap
Inventories: lower of the two

Cost versus net realizable value, where NRV is the selling price less completion and selling costs.

Trap
Gross profit only by function

The by-nature format runs from revenue straight to operating profit with no gross profit subtotal.

Trap
Direct depends on the cost object

The supervisor is indirect for the T-shirt and direct for the department. Never classify a resource before naming the cost object.

Trap
Product cost ≠ direct cost

Manufacturing overhead is indirect and still a product cost. Direct costs, on the other hand, are always product costs.

Trap
Variable means variable in total

Per unit a variable cost is constant and a fixed cost falls as volume grows — the opposite of the intuition the names suggest.

Trap
Job order still allocates overhead

It traces both direct components; only ABC changes how the indirect pool itself is spread.

Trap
Goodwill is tested, not amortized

Indefinite life means an impairment test every year, with the loss credited to the income statement.

Today. The loss is charged against the profit of the year, and goodwill is the one asset whose impairment can never be reversed.
